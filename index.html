Support Policy (MatrixTrack / Your App)

1. Introduction

At MatrixTrack, we are committed to providing timely, reliable, and efficient support to all our users. This Support Policy outlines how users can reach us, what kind of support is provided, and expected support practices.

2. Support Channels

Users can contact our support team through the following channels:

  • Email Support: it@apricitydigital.in
  • In-App Support (if available)
  • Website Contact Form

Support requests should include relevant details such as user ID, issue description, screenshots (if applicable), and contact information.

3. Support Hours

Our support team is available during:

  • Monday to Saturday: 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM (IST)
  • Sunday & Public Holidays: Limited or No Support

4. Types of Support Provided

We provide assistance for:

  • Account-related issues (login, access, etc.)
  • Technical issues or bugs
  • Data-related queries
  • Service usage guidance
  • Feature-related questions

5. User Responsibilities

To ensure faster resolution, users must:

  • Provide accurate and complete information
  • Avoid sharing false or misleading data
  • Follow troubleshooting steps suggested by support team
  • Maintain confidentiality of login credentials

6. Data & Privacy in Support

All user data shared during support interactions is handled securely and in accordance with our Privacy Policy. We do not share personal information with unauthorized third parties and only use it to resolve issues or improve services.

7. Limitations of Support

We may not provide support in the following cases:

  • Issues caused by third-party integrations not controlled by us
  • User negligence or misuse of the platform
  • Requests beyond the scope of our services

8. Escalation Policy

If your issue is not resolved:

  • You may request escalation via email
  • Escalated issues are reviewed by senior support staff
  • Resolution priority will be increased accordingly

9. Changes to Support Policy

We may update this Support Policy from time to time to reflect improvements in our services or legal requirements. Users are encouraged to review it periodically.

10. Contact Us

For any support-related queries, reach out to:

Email: it@apricitydigital.in
